# Landing: deviations from MASTER

| | |
|---|---|
| Surface | Main product and story landing page, ten sections (S01 to S10) |
| Audience | Potential customers, enterprise decision makers, judges and demo viewers, anyone new to the product |
| Dials | VARIANCE 7, MOTION 8, DENSITY 3 (authoritative in [MASTER §2.1](../MASTER.md)) |
| Specification | [landing-story.md](../../../docs/frontend/landing-story.md) (sections, copy, structure) and [landing-motion-map.md](../../../docs/frontend/landing-motion-map.md) (motion) |

Everything not listed below follows [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md) and its detail files unchanged: tokens, color roles, the single
typeface, the node grammar and the nine states, icons, radius, borders, content rules, accessibility floors and theme support.

## Deviations

| ID | MASTER rule | Default | On Landing | Justification | Bounds | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-01 | taste-rules rule 15 ("không hero"); MASTER §17 (centered hero) | No opening hero section | One opening section (S01): a category line, the headline "Đừng đợi khách hàng phải hỏi.", a sub-statement, the two doors, and a figure: an inert CareCard on a time axis that ends in one counterfactual marker | A landing page exists to orient a visitor who arrives cold; the owner briefs of 2026-10-03 require it and fix its core message | taste-skill §4.7: at 1280 by 720 everything fits the first viewport; at 390 by 844 the headline, sub-statement and doors fit and the figure follows; headline at most 2 lines at desktop; sub-statement at most 3 lines; the category line is the page's only eyebrow-like label; exactly two calls to action with the same labels used in S08 and S10; left-aligned asymmetric composition, never centered; no image except the real ComponentFigure and the node-grammar axis; no metrics, logos, testimonials or scroll cue; nothing animates on load | Blocked until amendment A-01 is merged |
| L-02 | taste-rules rule 10; [motion.md](../motion.md) §1 | Motion only signals state | Three narrative sequences, specified in [landing-motion-map.md](../../../docs/frontend/landing-motion-map.md): M1 the shift (S02 into S03), M2 the engine (S04), M3 the UC1 story (S05). Every other section is static apart from interaction feedback | MOTION 8: the product is a sequence in time, and scrubbing lets the reader control the pace of the explanation | [motion.md](../motion.md) §4 in full: scroll-scrubbed only, one moving focal element at a time, no hijacking, no parallax, no loops, no autoplay, time-based transitions at or below `--dur-slow`, nothing on load; only in the full tier (at least 1024px wide and 700px tall); complete static fallback under reduced motion, in smaller viewports, or without support; CSS scroll-driven animations or IntersectionObserver, no library | Blocked until amendment A-02 is merged |
| L-03 | [typography.md](../typography.md) §2 (largest heading `--text-4xl`) | h1 at most `--text-4xl` | `--text-display-lg` for the S01 headline; `--text-display` for at most one statement per viewport (S03 key line, S06 headline) | VARIANCE 7 without imagery: scale contrast carries the art direction | Weight 600, line height 1.15, `--tracking-display` only, one display element per viewport, headline at most 2 lines at desktop; diacritic test strings pass | Active |
| L-04 | [spacing.md](../spacing.md) §2 (comfortable) | Comfortable density | Airy density mode | DENSITY 3: one idea per screen for an unfamiliar audience | Target and text floors unchanged; running text within `--measure` | Active |
| L-05 | MASTER §13; [spacing.md](../spacing.md) §3.1 | Predictable, mostly single-axis layouts | Asymmetric splits (7 + 5, 8 + 4, offset starts) and a different layout family for every section; S02 and S03 share one sticky sequence and count as one family | VARIANCE 7; taste-skill §4.7 bans repeating a section layout | Every asymmetric section declares its single-column form below `md`; at most two image and text splits in a row; no bento grid; no three equal cards; the family of each section is listed in landing-story.md §2 | Active |
| L-06 | [content.md](../content.md) §4 (internal terms stay internal) | Internal system terms hidden | System terms appear where the story explains the mechanism (S04, S05, S06, S07): tín hiệu (signal), luật (rule), ứng viên (candidate), Agent, LLM, model, token, L0, L1, L2, Validator | Decision makers and judges evaluate the mechanism itself; the owner brief asks for "rules detect, agent reasons" and "not every event needs an LLM" | Each term carries a plain Vietnamese gloss on first use; terms never appear in S01 copy, door labels or calls to action | Active |
| L-07 | [components.md](../components.md) §1 (components are live and operable) | Components are interactive | Real components rendered inert as ComponentFigure illustrations | Show the actual product rather than imagery (MASTER §9 illustration policy) | `inert` and hidden from the accessibility tree, with a `figcaption` that describes the figure; visible "Dữ liệu mẫu" label; fixture data only; never a screenshot of a mock and never div drawings | Active |
| L-08 | MASTER §9; [components.md](../components.md) §1 (one real implementation, no lookalikes) | Figures show only real component states | Counterfactual elements that show what reactive care would have looked like: the S01 axis marker ("Không cần gửi"), the S02 reactive track, the rule-only outcome in S06 | The proactive idea is only visible against what it replaces (owner brief: S01 must make the concept obvious, S02 must show the reactive model) | Always labelled in words ("Nếu chờ khách hỏi", "Không cần gửi", "Chỉ có luật"); dashed `--border-strong` outline and `--muted` text; never interactive; never placed inside a ComponentFigure; never styled like a real message or state | Active |
| L-09 | MASTER §4 and [color.md](../color.md) §3.2 (color marks the present) | Only the current state and exceptions carry hue | Explanatory diagrams color every node by who holds the ball: the S01 axis, the S02 and S03 tracks, the S09 gate sequence | These diagrams exist to show who carries the burden; the pattern of holders is the message | Shapes and labels always present; any diagram with more than two node kinds carries a legend within the same viewport; ComponentFigures and the S04 stage rail keep "color marks the present" | Active |
