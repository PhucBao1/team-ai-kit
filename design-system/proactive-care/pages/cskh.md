# CSKH console: deviations from MASTER

| | |
|---|---|
| Surface | Customer-service employee console: overview, priority queue, active friction, case detail, customers, trace and audit |
| Audience | CSKH agents, service advisors (CVDV), the 24/7 line and approvers; many cases per shift, desktop stations |
| Dials | VARIANCE 3, MOTION 2, DENSITY 8 (authoritative in [MASTER §2.1](../MASTER.md)) |
| Screens | [cskh-screen-spec.md](../../../docs/frontend/cskh-screen-spec.md) (K1 to K7); structure [cskh-information-architecture.md](../../../docs/frontend/cskh-information-architecture.md); motion [cskh-motion-map.md](../../../docs/frontend/cskh-motion-map.md); inventory [screen-map §5](../../../docs/frontend/screen-map.md) |

Everything not listed below follows [MASTER.md](../MASTER.md) and its detail files unchanged: tokens, color roles, the single
typeface, the node grammar and the nine states with their labels and icons, radius, content rules, accessibility floors and theme support.
The Demo stage's DemoPanel and AgentTrace follow this page too.

## Deviations

| ID | MASTER rule | Default | On CSKH | Justification | Bounds | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K-01 | [spacing.md](../spacing.md) §2 (comfortable) | Comfortable density | Compact density mode | DENSITY 8: a handoff must be understood in about 10 seconds (proposal §08), so more of the case is visible at once | Targets stay at least `--target-min` with `--target-gap`; text at least `--text-sm`; rows separated by hairlines | Active |
| K-02 | [typography.md](../typography.md) §2 (body `--text-md`) | Running text and data at `--text-md` | `--text-sm` for data cells, metadata, chips, LayerTags and timestamps | Dense scanning of structured data | Narrative text (summaries, first lines of case sections, notes, the HandoffCard briefing) stays `--text-md`; never below `--text-sm`; line height stays 1.6; numbers tabular | Active |
| K-03 | [motion.md](../motion.md) §2 (default catalogue) | Customer motion catalogue | The employee's own actions never animate: no press scale, no enter, rise or stagger, no trace node animation, no settle; hover, popovers, menus and keyboard-opened disclosures are instant. Changes the server makes while an element is on screen get one opacity cue: the row change mark (`--sunken` overlay fading over `--dur-slow`, plus a static "Vừa đổi" time), the update control fading in, or a StateChip, status line or verification segment change over `--dur-fast`. Pointer-opened disclosures rotate their chevron over `--dur-fast`; dialogs fade over `--dur-fast` | MOTION 2. emil-design-eng's frequency rule: the employee's own actions repeat hundreds of times a shift, and keyboard actions never animate. Changes arriving from the server must still be noticed in peripheral vision without anything moving | Allowed motion is exactly this list plus the EXECUTING loader; opacity and the chevron's rotation only, never position, size or order; at most one cue per element per update, and none when one update changes more than six elements (static marks instead); no cue on load, on navigation or for the employee's own action; under reduced motion every cue is the static "Vừa đổi" mark | Active |
| K-04 | MASTER §2.1 (VARIANCE 5); [typography.md](../typography.md) §2 | Offset compositions allowed; h1 up to `--text-4xl` | Strict pane grid, no offset compositions; largest heading `--text-2xl`; the case summary `h1` at `--text-xl` | VARIANCE 3: predictability is speed | Pane order fixed (queue, case, decision); case section order fixed (cskh-screen-spec.md K4); full-width screens on a 12-column grid | Active |
| K-05 | MASTER §7 (a distinct object may earn a box) | Cards for distinct objects | No cards; panes, hairlines and space only | taste-skill §4.4 bans generic card containers above DENSITY 7 | Floating layers (menus, dialogs, tooltips) still use elevation | Active |
| K-06 | [content.md](../content.md) §4 (internal terms stay internal) | Internal system terms hidden | Pipeline, layer, tool, rule and event terms visible in case sections 1, 2, 4 and 6, in the Active Friction table and in Trace and Audit | Staff must be able to audit why the AI acted (proposal §09: explainability, audit trail) | The case header, the first line of every section and the HandoffCard briefing stay in plain language; every term has a glossary tooltip | Active |
| K-07 | MASTER §10 (keyboard); [accessibility.md](../accessibility.md) §2 (2.1.4) | No single-key shortcuts | The shortcut set in [accessibility.md](../accessibility.md) §6 | Speed for repeated work | Active only with focus in the queue or case regions and not in a text field; a switch turns them off; every shortcut has a visible control; no single-key shortcut for consequential decisions | Active |
| K-08 | MASTER §13 (mobile-first) | Mobile-first | Desktop-first, designed for `xl` | Staff work at desktop stations with large screens | Still reflows down to 320px, one pane at a time with back navigation, no horizontal page scroll ([spacing.md](../spacing.md) §3.3) | Active |
| K-09 | MASTER §10 (deadlines, not countdowns); taste-rules rule 9 | No countdowns | DeadlineTimer shows the minutes remaining on a callback deadline | Staff owe a callback the agent promised; remaining time is operational information, not pressure on a customer | Never shown to customers; text updates once a minute with no animation; colors per MASTER §11; announced only at thresholds | Active |
| K-10 | MASTER §10 (summary first) | Details behind disclosures | All ten case sections and every EvidenceList expanded by default | Staff must never miss evidence, a promise or a "Không nên" item | The section index provides navigation; only raw tool payloads may collapse, with their size; checks that did not pass are always named, never counted away | Active |
| K-11 | MASTER §11 (connection banners); [components.md](../components.md) §10 Banner | Page-level banners for connection and safety push the content down | A fixed-width alert slot in the header carries safety and connection status; panes never shift | VARIANCE 3 and misclick safety: a banner that pushes three panes down can move a consequential button under the pointer | Safety is still pinned first in every queue view and announced with `role="alert"`; the slot uses words and an icon, never color alone; offline also replaces every action with its reason; with several alerts safety wins and "+1" opens the list; the Demo stage banner stays a banner | Active |
| K-12 | [components.md](../components.md) §10 AppHeader (ThemeSwitch in the header) | Theme switch in the header | Theme switch in the account menu | At `xl` the one-line header holds five destinations, customer search and the alert slot | One step from every screen; default "Theo máy"; the account menu is keyboard reachable | Active |
